# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Offline Android alarm scheduling with repeat days and individual enable switches.
- QR challenge dismissal or standard dismiss and five-minute snooze per alarm.
- Ten bundled CC0 alarm sounds, short previews, and private local audio imports.
- Light and dark appearances, QR export, permission guidance, and draft recovery.
- Native restoration of future alarms after reboot, time changes, and app updates.

Device verification is required before publishing a release APK; see
`docs/verification.md` and `docs/public-release.md`.
